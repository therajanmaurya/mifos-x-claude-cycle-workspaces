Mifos X Field Officer is the mobile app for loan officers and field staff at microfinance institutions, credit unions and banks that run Mifos X / Apache Fineract. Visit clients, open accounts, disburse and collect loans, and record savings deposits from the field, even where there is no network.

Built by the Mifos Initiative, a nonprofit working on financial inclusion for the world's unbanked, it connects to your institution's own Fineract server. Your institution holds the data; the app is the field officer's window into it.

WORK OFFLINE, SYNC LATER
• Choose the centres, groups and clients you will visit, and the app downloads everything you need before you leave the office.
• Keep working with no connection. Actions you take offline are saved as drafts.
• When you are back in coverage, Sync & Drafts uploads them to the server.

YOUR DAY ON ONE SCREEN
• A home dashboard with today's collections, your work queue, meetings and your portfolio.
• Every card follows your Fineract permissions, so you only see what you are allowed to act on.

CLIENTS
• Register new clients with their personal details, identifiers, photo and signature.
• View, edit and activate clients, and attach documents and notes.
• Search across clients, groups, centres and accounts.

LOANS
• Create loan applications and follow them through approval and disbursement.
• Record repayments and view each loan's schedule, charges and transactions.

SAVINGS AND RECURRING DEPOSITS
• Open savings and recurring deposit accounts.
• Record deposits and withdrawals, and view balances and history.

CENTRES, GROUPS AND COLLECTION SHEETS
• Manage centres and groups and their members.
• Run individual and group collection sheets for a meeting in one pass.

REPORTS AND APPROVALS
• Run your institution's reports from the device.
• Work through the checker inbox to approve or reject pending tasks.

SECURE BY DESIGN
• Sign in with the credentials your institution issues.
• Data is sent only to your institution's server, over an encrypted connection.
• The offline copy on the device is encrypted and cleared when you sign out.

This app needs a Mifos X / Apache Fineract account from your institution. Mifos X Field Officer is free and open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. Source code, issues and contributor guides: github.com/openMF/mifos-x-field-officer-app
