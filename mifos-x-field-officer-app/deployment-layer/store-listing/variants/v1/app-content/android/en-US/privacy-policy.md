# Privacy Policy — Mifos X Field Officer

**Last updated**: November 4, 2026

## 1. Who we are

Mifos X Field Officer ("the app") is a free, open-source app published by The Mifos Initiative ("we", "us"), a nonprofit working on financial inclusion. It is used by the staff of microfinance institutions, credit unions and banks that run Mifos X / Apache Fineract.

This policy covers the app only. Our website and other services are covered by the [Mifos Initiative Privacy Policy](https://mifos.org/privacy-policy/).

## 2. The short version

- You sign in with an account **your institution** gives you. The app does nothing without it.
- Client and account records go **only to your institution's own server**, which you choose when you sign in. **We do not receive, store or see them.**
- Records you sync for offline work stay **encrypted on your device** and are removed when you sign out.
- **Usage analytics and crash reports are off** until you agree to them — you are asked during setup, and you can change your answer at any time in **Settings → Privacy**.
- There are **no ads**, no advertising profile, no in-app purchases, and the app does **not** use your location.

## 3. Who controls the records you work with

Your institution decides what is recorded about its clients and accounts, holds those records on its own Fineract server, and is responsible for them under its own privacy policy and the law that applies to it. **For any question about a client or account record — including access, correction or deletion — contact the institution that holds it.** We cannot see or change those records.

## 4. What the app sends to your institution's server

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your username and password | To sign you in. The password is sent over an encrypted connection and kept in the device's secure storage. |
| Client details — names, date of birth, gender, phone, email, address, identification numbers and documents | To register and manage your institution's clients |
| Client photos and signatures | Only when you add them, using the device camera or files you choose |
| Documents you attach | To store them with the client or account |
| Loans, savings and recurring deposits — applications, approvals, disbursements, repayments, deposits, withdrawals, charges | To carry out and record the work of your institution |

All of this travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and your institution's permissions decide what your account can see and change.

## 5. Information stored on your device

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Records of the centres, groups and clients you choose to sync | So you can work without a connection | Encrypted on-device database |
| Actions you take while offline | Kept as drafts until **Sync & Drafts** uploads them | Encrypted on-device database |
| Your sign-in session and the server you use | To keep you signed in | Device secure storage |
| Your settings, including your privacy choices | To remember them | On-device settings |

Signing out removes the records the app stored on the device.

## 6. Usage analytics and crash reports (only if you agree)

The app includes **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**. Both stay **off** until you agree to them during setup or in **Settings → Privacy**, and you can withdraw that agreement at any time.

If you agree:

- **Crash reports** describe what went wrong in the app (the device model, operating-system version, app version and the error) so we can fix it.
- **Usage analytics** count which screens and features are used, so we know what to improve.
- Neither includes client or account records. No advertising identifier is used for advertising.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 7. What the app does not do

- It does **not** access your location. Path tracking only shows routes already recorded on your institution's server.
- It does **not** read your contacts, messages, calendar or other apps' data.
- It shows **no ads** and does not build an advertising profile.
- It does **not** sell or share personal information with anyone for a business or commercial purpose.

## 8. How long data is kept

Records on your institution's server are kept under your institution's retention policy. On the device, synced records and drafts are kept until you sign out or they are uploaded. If you agreed to them, crash reports and analytics data are kept by Firebase for no longer than needed to fix and improve the app, and never more than six months after you withdraw your agreement, unless the law requires otherwise.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada, South Africa, and US states such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Virginia — you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

- For **client and account records**, contact your institution (section 3).
- For **analytics and crash reports**, switch them off in **Settings → Privacy**, or contact us (section 13).
- In the EEA, UK or Switzerland you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, the offline copy is encrypted, and access is limited by the permissions your institution sets. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that it will never be breached.

## 11. Children

The app is a work tool for employees of financial institutions. It is not directed at, and is not intended for, anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 12. Open source and changes

The app's complete source code is public at <https://github.com/openMF/mifos-x-field-officer-app>, so anyone can check how it handles data. If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
